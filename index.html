    01 — Cold start

    It only wakes if you don’t let go.

    The Warden idles at 0.3% reactor draw, just enough to keep the gyros warm. It has no autopilot and no override. What you just did, holding until the lights came on, is the only way to start it.

    1. T+0.0Ignition. You press and the core starts to come up. Let go and it stops.
    2. T+3.4Handshake. Forty-one actuators report in, heel to crown, in 0.6 s.
    3. T+9.2Sync. The harness calibrates to you. Nobody else is steering.
